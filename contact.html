Questions

Contact FAQ

How long does VDRSA take to respond?
Most messages are answered within 3 working days. Grievances involving child safety are prioritized.
Can I raise a grievance anonymously?
Yes. Select "Grievance" and tick "Keep my identity confidential" — your name will not be shared with the parties involved.
Who handles media requests?
Select "Media" and describe your request; the communications desk will respond with accreditation and coverage details.
How do I apply for club recognition?
Select "Club recognition" and share your club's details; the office will send the application form and checklist.
Is my personal information kept private?
Yes. Contact details are used only to respond to your message and are never published or shared without consent.
