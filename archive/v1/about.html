About VDRSA

The district body governing roller sports in Visakhapatnam — competitions, club recognition, athlete registration and coach development.

Who we are

A district body for every skater

The Visakhapatnam District Roller Sports Association (VDRSA) is the recognized district-level body for roller sports — speed skating, artistic skating, inline hockey, inline freestyle and skateboarding — in Visakhapatnam district.

We organize district championships and selection trials, recognize and support skating clubs, register athletes on a single Skater ID, and develop coaches and officials — all under the technical rules of our state and national federations.

What drives us

Mission, vision & values

Mission

To grow roller sports across Visakhapatnam district by giving every skater a fair, safe and well-run pathway from first lesson to district champion.

Vision

A district where roller sports are part of every school and neighbourhood, and where Visakhapatnam skaters compete confidently at state and national level.

Values

Fair play, athlete safety, transparent selection, and clubs and coaches held to one common standard.
