Privacy

What we show, and what we never show

We show

  • Masked athlete name (e.g. A. K****)
  • Club name
  • Discipline and category
  • Validity status and valid-till date

We never show

  • Photograph
  • Date of birth
  • Phone number or address
  • Parent or guardian details
Questions

Verification FAQ

Who can use this verification tool?
Anyone — clubs, event officials, schools, media and parents — can verify a Skater ID without signing in.
Why is the athlete's name masked?
To protect the privacy of skaters, most of whom are minors, only an initial and first letter of the surname are shown.
What does "expired" mean?
The ID was valid in a previous season but the club has not yet renewed the athlete's registration for the current season.
I can't find an ID — what should I do?
Check the format (VDRSA-YY-NNNNN). If it still doesn't verify, contact the athlete's club or the VDRSA office.
Can I verify an ID on behalf of a club for event entry?
Yes. Event organizers routinely verify IDs at entry checks; only the fields shown above are needed to confirm eligibility.
